# NEAR Lesson 3: Wallet & Intents

**Duration:** 1.5 hours · **Format:** hands-on + short concept talk

## Lesson Goal

Create a NEAR wallet, deposit a token, and grasp **Intents** — cross-chain swaps where you say *what* you want and solvers figure out *how*.

## Part 1 — What NEAR is (5 min)

NEAR is a fast, cheap layer-1 blockchain built for usability — human-readable account names (e.g. `alice.near`) and a focus on **chain abstraction**: making other chains usable from one place.

## Part 2 — Create a wallet & deposit (30 min)

1. Go to **near.com** (or a NEAR wallet of your choice).
2. Create an account — pick a human-readable name.
3. Deposit a small amount of a token.

## Part 3 — NEAR Intents (30 min)

**Intents** = you express *what you want* ("swap USDC for NEAR"), and **solvers** compete to fulfill it. No manual bridging, no wrapping tokens.

- Cross-chain by design: assets from many chains settle through Intents.
- You don't hold wrapped tokens; the solver settles on the destination chain.

**Do:** visit the NEAR Intents app (linked from near.com) and do a small cross-chain swap.

## Homework

1. Create a NEAR wallet and deposit a small amount.
2. Complete one Intents swap.
3. Write 3 sentences: what's the difference between a normal swap and an Intent?

## Troubleshooting

- **Swap slow** → Intents are solver-matched; wait a minute and check the explorer.

## Result

A funded NEAR wallet + you can explain Intents in one sentence.
