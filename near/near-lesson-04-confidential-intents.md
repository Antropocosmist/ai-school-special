# NEAR Lesson 4: Confidential Intents

**Duration:** 1.5 hours · **Format:** concept talk + reading + discussion

## Lesson Goal

Understand **confidential Intents** — private cross-chain money — and the **NEAR × ZCash** story behind them.

## Part 1 — Read the source (30 min)

Read the official post:

**https://www.near.org/blog/confidential-intents**

## Part 2 — What "confidential" adds (25 min)

Regular Intents (Lesson 3) settle swaps, but your amounts and identity can be observed. **Confidential Intents** add privacy: the *what* and the *who* of your swap aren't revealed.

Key ideas:

- **"Confidential Intents create private money."**
- You can move value across chains without exposing your amounts or identity.

## Part 3 — NEAR × ZCash (25 min)

NEAR Intents unlocked **ZCash** — the two are now closely tied:

- The ZCash token address viewed on CoinGecko is a **NEAR smart-contract address** — a concrete example of chain abstraction: a private currency integrated through NEAR.

**Discuss in the group:** why does privacy matter for real-world money, and what trade-offs does it bring (regulatory, usability)?

## Homework

1. Summarize the blog post in 5 sentences.
2. Write down one concrete use case where confidential Intents matter.

## Result

You can explain confidential Intents and why NEAR × ZCash is significant.
